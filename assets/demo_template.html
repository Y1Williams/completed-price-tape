Cost feedback records one scalar execution cost. Completed tape feedback also records prices throughout execution and recovery. Information here means Fisher information about the unknown recovery parameter θ.

Model: T = Q = σ = 1, R = 2, τ = 0. The recovery density is gθ(β) = (2/3)[1 + θ(5 − 4β)/3], with β ∈ [0.5, 2] and θ ∈ [−0.5, 0.5]. Here U is the recovery window, T is the execution horizon and R limits the trading rate.

How to interpret the two numerical objects

The line uses analytical inequalities over the continuous control class, evaluated by quadrature. The circles use feasible designs found by deterministic searches from several initial schedules. Their derivations and numerical status are documented in docs/METHODS.md. Display rounding and the numerical padding do not establish a rigorous floating point enclosure.

The displayed multiplier is an information comparison for this example. A numerical information ratio alone does not verify the regularity assumptions needed for a decision complexity claim about this family.
